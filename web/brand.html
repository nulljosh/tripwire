The mark

Colour

Each chip: light on the left, dark on the right. Red is the only colour Tripwire owns: the accent, the buttons, the frame and the full-bleed band. Green and red inside a diff mean added and removed, nothing else.

The system font, nothing to load. SF on Apple devices, Helvetica or Arial elsewhere. Headlines are bold and tracked tight. Code and diffs use the system monospace.
