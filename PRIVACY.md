# tripwire Privacy Policy

*Last updated September 2026*

tripwire is an API and CI watcher. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What leaves your device
- **Your account.** Sign-in and the things you post are stored in our database on Supabase. You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it.
- **What we watch.** The endpoints and repos you add are stored so we can check them. Alerts go out by email through Resend. Payments go through Stripe; we never see your card.
- **Site analytics.** The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
tripwire isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
